Read this as three groups:
  • particles with no English counterpart (H853 direct-object marker, and
    the like) — correctly unaligned, and no threshold will change that
  • "(never seen in the OT)" — NT-only vocabulary; the model cannot align
    what it has no training example of
  • content words with a sensible top English — these are the real misses,
    and lowering --min-prob (currently 0.04) will pick them up
